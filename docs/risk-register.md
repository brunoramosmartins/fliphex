| R8 | Compute constraint on the G7 laptop | 3 | 2 | **6** | Network sized for this hardware. CPU MCTS + GPU inference. Optionally rent cloud time for the final tuned run only. **Measured 2026-08-31.** No GPU is reachable (462-series Windows driver predates WSL2 CUDA), so this is CPU-only, and self-play parallelises **4.31×**, not the order of magnitude first assumed — 6 physical cores, and 6 workers already return only 3.26×. Even so the composed rate is ~705 games/hour, and the chosen schedule (30 generations × 200 games, gate every 5) puts H3's five seeds at **59.6 h**. Affordable, so no deviation is registered against the pre-registered seed count. Likelihood drops 2 → **1**: the binding numbers are now measured rather than estimated. Residual: the parallel scaling was measured engine-only and torch has not yet competed for those cores. **The residual is closed 2026-09-04 (EXP-014) and the risk materialised.** The composed rate, with the network in the loop, is **145 games/hour** against the 705 above — the binding number was wrong by **4.9×**. **The error was compounded twice, and reconstructing the 59.6 h shows how.** That figure is 42,000 games ÷ 705: the run's 30,000 self-play games **plus the gate's 12,000**, all priced at the *parallel self-play* rate. The gate did not parallelise at all — it ran in one process at **89 games/hour**, four times the per-game cost of parallel self-play, and was **61%** of a projected **220.9 h**. **A correction to this row's own reasoning, not only to its numbers.** "The binding numbers are now measured rather than estimated" was not a valid reason to lower a likelihood, because the measurement omitted both the network and the dominant cost. And the residual it *did* name was the right worry pointed at the wrong quantity: the parallel **scaling** was roughly right — engine-only predicted 3.26× at six workers and the composed measurement gives **3.76×** — while the per-game **cost** was 2.1× worse for self-play (5.10 s → 10.87 s) and 2.7× for the gate at its own optimum. Scaling was fine; cost was not. **Partly repaired 2026-09-04**: the gate now plays in a spawned pool, verified identical to the serial match, which brings the projection to **137.6 h** — 83 hours recovered without touching scope, and still **2.3×** the budget, so a schedule amendment is due. Worker counts are now measured rather than assumed and they differ by activity: self-play saturates at **six** (eight within 1%), the gate peaks at **four** and loses 33% at eight, because a gate worker holds two networks against self-play's one. Likelihood rises 1 → **3**: this is no longer a risk that might bite, the registered schedule does not fit the machine. Residual: the gate's early saturation is explained from structure and **not measured**. | Materialised; gate parallelised, schedule amendment due |
