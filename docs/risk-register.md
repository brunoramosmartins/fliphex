| R6 | AZ training unstable or non-convergent | 3 | 2 | **6** | Small net, small game. Multiple seeds distinguish intrinsic instability from bad luck. Shrink the network before growing it. **Measured 2026-09-16 (EXP-015) and the risk materialised.** Five independent seeds, 30 generations each, 135.0 h: **four clear the prior-free UCT floor and one does not** — seed 4 at **56.0% [49.1%, 62.7%]** against a bar of 114 wins in 200, missing by two games. Per the entry's pre-registered falsifiability clause that is recorded as instability and is not averaged away, so **H3's clause 1 is not satisfied**. **The failure is not one unlucky seed, and this row's own mitigation is what established that.** The five rates are 64.0%, 76.0%, 63.0%, 56.0%, 62.0%: a between-seed `sd` of **7.3%** against the **3.4%** the 200-game samples alone would produce, and a homogeneity `χ²` of **18.52** on 4 df against a 0.05 critical value of 9.488. A single underlying win rate is rejected — runs differing only in seed reach genuinely different strengths, and 56% and 76% are both things this pipeline produces. "Multiple seeds distinguish intrinsic instability from bad luck" is the one mitigation in this register that did exactly what it said, and it was load-bearing: at one seed this is invisible, and at five without the homogeneity read it is arguable. **Three corrections to this row's own text.** First, the risk is worded as *"unstable **or** non-convergent"* and only the first half bit: every seed converged, policy loss falling 5.05–5.20 → 3.74–3.88 on all five, value loss 0.49–0.61 → 0.42–0.44, and every trained prior beats its own generation-0 network by **45 to 62.5 points**. Second, *"small net, small game"* was an argument for why this was unlikely, and it is now falsified as such: 347,887 parameters on 25 cells was unstable anyway, so size was never the protection it was offered as. And **no remedy was attempted**, by design — retraining until seed 4 clears would select on the outcome and requires a new registered entry. | Materialised; measured, unexplained, no remedy attempted |
| R14 | **The learner does not reach the standard H3 sets** — not weak for any one fixable reason, simply not good enough on the shipped game | 3 | 2 | **6** | **Added 2026-09-17, after the fact, and the lateness is the point.** This register held thirteen risks and every one of them named a *cause* that could make the learner weak — head architecture (R5), action aliasing (R13), no augmentation (R7), compute (R8), training instability (R6) — and **none named the outcome**. H3's own falsification condition had no row. The nearest, R9, is scoped to a disagreement on the **3×3 value** at likelihood 1 and frames the case as "would be a real finding, not a failure"; it cannot carry "the champion preserves 75.7% of won positions where 90% was required". R6 does not cover it either, by its own text: *"what varies is the level reached, not whether training proceeds"* — a run whose five seeds all landed on 75.7% with an `sd` of 0.2% would satisfy R6 completely and still fail H3. **Materialised on arrival, 2026-09-17 (EXP-006).** All five EXP-015 champions, 400 simulations, 352 exactly solved won positions from the shipped 5×5 at `k ≤ 8`: **74.4%, 77.0%, 75.9%, 75.6%, 75.6%**, every Wilson interval far below the pre-registered 0.90 — the best seed's **upper** limit is 81.1%, nine points short. The ladder is the useful reading: random mover **21.9%**, raw prior **53.5%**, champion with search **75.7%**, required **90.0%**. The learner is emphatically doing something (+53.8 points over random, +22.2 for search over the prior); it is 14 points from the standard. **Why this is a risk and not merely a result.** The result is fine — a pre-registered bar that is missed and reported is worth more than one that is met. What the missing row cost is *foresight*: nobody had written down what would happen if the learner simply fell short, so there was no pre-declared response, and the register offered five causal stories any of which could be reached for after the fact. Naming the outcome separately from its causes is what stops that. Likelihood **3**, because it has happened. Impact **2** and deliberately not 3: the project's thesis requires H3 to be *answered*, not to be true; the verdict is measured, bounded and reportable, and no deliverable is lost. The counter-argument — that a negative verdict on the central hypothesis of one of three axes is a 3 — is recorded and not taken. **Three residuals.** The **cause is unattributed**, by design: nothing in EXP-006 varies architecture, budget or training length, so attributing the 14 points to any of R5, R6, R7, R8 or R13 would be a story rather than a measurement. The **threshold was never derived** — 0.90 was pre-declared on 2026-08-05 without an argument from anything measurable; it does not matter at a 14-point gap and it will matter to the next entry that lands near it. And the measurement covers **endgames only**, `k ≤ 8` at three depths on one machine. | Materialised on arrival; cause unattributed by design |

| R8 | Compute constraint on the G7 laptop | 3 | 2 | **6** | Network sized for this hardware. CPU MCTS + GPU inference. Optionally rent cloud time for the final tuned run only. **Measured 2026-08-31.** No GPU is reachable (462-series Windows driver predates WSL2 CUDA), so this is CPU-only, and self-play parallelises **4.31×**, not the order of magnitude first assumed — 6 physical cores, and 6 workers already return only 3.26×. Even so the composed rate is ~705 games/hour, and the chosen schedule (30 generations × 200 games, gate every 5) puts H3's five seeds at **59.6 h**. Affordable, so no deviation is registered against the pre-registered seed count. Likelihood drops 2 → **1**: the binding numbers are now measured rather than estimated. Residual: the parallel scaling was measured engine-only and torch has not yet competed for those cores. **The residual is closed 2026-09-04 (EXP-014) and the risk materialised.** The composed rate, with the network in the loop, is **145 games/hour** against the 705 above — the binding number was wrong by **4.9×**. **The error was compounded twice, and reconstructing the 59.6 h shows how.** That figure is 42,000 games ÷ 705: the run's 30,000 self-play games **plus the gate's 12,000**, all priced at the *parallel self-play* rate. The gate did not parallelise at all — it ran in one process at **89 games/hour**, four times the per-game cost of parallel self-play, and was **61%** of a projected **220.9 h**. **A correction to this row's own reasoning, not only to its numbers.** "The binding numbers are now measured rather than estimated" was not a valid reason to lower a likelihood, because the measurement omitted both the network and the dominant cost. And the residual it *did* name was the right worry pointed at the wrong quantity: the parallel **scaling** was roughly right — engine-only predicted 3.26× at six workers and the composed measurement gives **3.76×** — while the per-game **cost** was 2.1× worse for self-play (5.10 s → 10.87 s) and 2.7× for the gate at its own optimum. Scaling was fine; cost was not. **Partly repaired 2026-09-04**: the gate now plays in a spawned pool, verified identical to the serial match, which brings the projection to **137.6 h** — 83 hours recovered without touching scope, and still **2.3×** the budget, so a schedule amendment is due. Worker counts are now measured rather than assumed and they differ by activity: self-play saturates at **six** (eight within 1%), the gate peaks at **four** and loses 33% at eight, because a gate worker holds two networks against self-play's one. Likelihood rises 1 → **3**: this is no longer a risk that might bite, the registered schedule does not fit the machine. Residual: the gate's early saturation is explained from structure and **not measured**. **The schedule amendment is discharged, 2026-09-16.** It was never written as an amendment because EXP-015's registration carried the measured cost table from the start — 29 h/seed, ≈146 h total — so the run was registered against the repaired number rather than the broken one. It came in at **135.0 h, 27.0 h per seed**: the projection was 8% high, and it is the first cost estimate in this project to land. It landed because it was built from EXP-014's measured decomposition instead of from a composed rate, which is the concrete lesson this row was missing. Likelihood stays at **3**: the machine is still the binding constraint and the next large run will meet it again. | Materialised; gate parallelised, schedule discharged |
